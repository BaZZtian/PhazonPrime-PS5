Third-party tools retain their individual terms. The grant covers only controlled rights and makes no exclusive human-copyright claim for every AI-assisted fragment.

The shipped DatSketch Playstation5 Digital Edition model, unchanged GLB and disclosed technical runtime representation are separately CC BY-ND4.0 and are excluded from these project-controlled MIT grants. The generic generator is an alternative source tool; the shipped model is not its project-created output. See console-model-NOTICE.txt and console-model-CC-BY-ND-4.0.txt for attribution, transformations and original terms. The public adaptation question is not resolved by an own-code grant.

Metaforce/Aurora and all other third-party work retain their original grants. Existing native platform/input/encoder GPL obligations remain. No blanket relicensing is asserted.
